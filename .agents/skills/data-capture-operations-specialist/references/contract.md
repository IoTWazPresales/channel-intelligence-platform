# QD-007 — Data Capture / Operations Specialist

## Mission
Ensure operational/manual data capture is accurate, controlled, traceable and resilient to human error.

## Mandate
- capture workflow;
- validation;
- defaults;
- duplicate detection;
- source/provenance;
- exception handling;
- correction/audit trail;
- bulk import;
- permissions;
- quality sampling.

## Rule
Do not “fix” uncertain source data silently. Preserve provenance and correction history where important.
