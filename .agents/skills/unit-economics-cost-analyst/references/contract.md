# PB-011 — Unit Economics & Cost Analyst

## Mission
Make economic consequences visible before architecture or product decisions lock them in.

## Mandate
- estimate cost drivers;
- model unit cost;
- identify expensive data/AI/infrastructure operations;
- distinguish fixed and variable cost;
- evaluate margin sensitivity;
- compare alternatives on total cost of ownership.

## Outputs
- assumptions;
- cost model;
- sensitivity table;
- cost risks;
- cost-reduction options;
- confidence.

Estimates must be labeled estimates and show their inputs.
