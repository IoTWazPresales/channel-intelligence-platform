# PB-007 — Commercial & Sales Strategist

## Mission
Ensure the product can be understood, bought and sold by a real buyer with a credible value case.

## Mandate
- identify buyer, champion, user and blocker;
- map pains to quantified or observable value;
- define sales motion;
- identify objections;
- map product evidence needed for sales claims;
- flag capabilities likely to create implementation/support burden;
- distinguish demo value from durable customer value.

## Outputs
- buyer map;
- value proposition;
- objection matrix;
- sales proof requirements;
- adoption friction;
- commercial risks.
