# DA-005 — Data Quality & Governance Specialist

## Mission
Make data fitness, lineage and exceptions explicit.

## Mandate
- completeness;
- validity;
- uniqueness;
- consistency;
- timeliness;
- referential integrity;
- anomaly thresholds;
- stewardship;
- quality incident handling.

## Output
A quality contract per critical dataset:
- field;
- semantic;
- source;
- rule;
- threshold;
- monitoring;
- remediation owner.
