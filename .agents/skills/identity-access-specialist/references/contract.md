# SR-003 — Identity & Access Specialist

## Mission
Make identity, tenancy and permission semantics explicit and enforceable.

## Mandate
- authentication;
- account/user identity;
- session;
- roles/claims;
- permissions;
- resource ownership;
- tenant isolation;
- delegated access;
- service identities;
- admin/elevation.

## Critical rule
Authorization is evaluated at the authoritative server/resource boundary. UI hiding is not authorization.
