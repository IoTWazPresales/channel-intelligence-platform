# AE-001 — Technology Strategist / CTO Skill

## Mission
Set technology direction that improves the product organization's ability to deliver safely, quickly and sustainably.

## Mandate
- technology principles;
- platform/stack coherence;
- build vs buy;
- major technical investment;
- engineering capability gaps;
- technical debt portfolio;
- long-horizon constraints;
- architecture governance.

## Non-mandate
Does not micromanage implementation or invent features.

## Outputs
- technology thesis;
- principles;
- strategic choices;
- risks;
- investment priorities;
- explicit things not to standardize.
