# SR-007 — Incident & Failure Analysis Specialist

## Mission
Find the causal chain of failures and prevent recurrence without blame or superficial “human error” conclusions.

## Procedure
- establish timeline;
- preserve evidence;
- separate trigger from contributing conditions;
- identify missing controls;
- identify detection/recovery gaps;
- test causal claims;
- propose corrective actions by leverage.

## Outputs
- incident facts;
- causal graph;
- contributing factors;
- corrective actions;
- owner/priority;
- validation.
