FINDINGS, RANKED
Blocker for E7 acceptance
1. Shipped quantity double counts closed lines. Scenario: a PO-less shipped twin retired as
   closed_superseded, or an open order closed as closed_shipped, keeps its ship confirm date and is
   counted beside the shipped row that replaces it. Size on cip today: +5,612 units and +76 lines
   (closed_superseded 3,597; closed_shipped 2,015). Other readers already exclude these states
   (supply_overview.py:102; comment at shipment_shipped_fact_identity_twin_merge.py:488).
   Fix direction: count line_state = 'shipped' only, or exclude the four closed states.
Major
2. Per-week totals are wrong on any non-platform calendar when no date window is set: one Sunday week
   shows 14/wk in its row and 7/wk in the total. Affects KPI and total displays (calc.py:34-52).
3. Lineup and sell-in allow day and week grains on period-start dates. Lineup by week puts a quarter's
   plan into its first week. On cip, 32 of 32 dated cases start on the 1st.
4. A hidden or narrowed sellout_units still exposes dsi_sellout.priced_units: the overlay intent is
   bypassed for 44% of units. Not a row-data leak.
Minor
5. The catalog offers grains and breakdowns the planner refuses (CST day, open-shipment period), and
   requires is empty for prior year and running total.
6. Pending measures are refused as "Unknown measure" instead of with their pending reason.
7. On-hand for a product missing from the latest report comes back as a null row, and a null total
   when filtered, rather than 0.
8. _legacy_anchor checks only the first legacy input of a calculated measure. Latent: no current
   measure has two different anchors.
9. catalog_for_tenant_cached is a process-local lru_cache with no TTL. A worker process keeps old
   hidden flags until it restarts. From reading the code; not run.
10. Possible: with a window longer than a year that opens mid-bucket, a later bucket's prior year may
    be the partial first bucket. From reading the code; not run.
11. Observation: cip alembic reads 20261101_0050, which differs from CURRENT.md's "cip at 0048"
    (ASSERTED doc vs VERIFIED query).
