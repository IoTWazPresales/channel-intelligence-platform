MAJOR FINDINGS

1. Closed states are still counted by readers that do not exclude them.
   Verified by code and cip counts; the endpoints were not executed against cip.
   - twin_remainder (shipment_strict_pair.py:218-247) only looks at shipped and open_order rows. A closed row falls through as kept, at its full quantity, so any reader that relies on twin.kept counts it.
   - channel_ops /cover-distribution open inbound (channel_ops.py:1134-1150): 1,157 closed rows / 79,112 units on cip, against 668 / 45,316 that are not closed.
   - /movement-lens (channel_ops.py:246-256), /forecasts (channel_ops.py:1013-1024), and the dashboards v2 semantic model shipments.quantity and lines (semantics/models/default.yaml:289-314) all count:
     - 47 closed_superseded rows / 3,597 units, which double-count their PO twins;
     - 29 closed_shipped rows / 2,015 units.
   - cpor/intake_weighted_mac.py: 820 closed rows / 59,712 units weight the intake cost.

2. The 0050 downgrade (20261101_0050_fact_superseded.py:32) sets every closed_superseded row to shipped, including orphan-retired open orders.
   On cip that is 9 rows / 921 units of former open_order lines with no confirm date. Verified by P10.

3. An undated complete-book file still writes facts.
   job_is_older_than_applied_book returns False when there is no date.
   - P4: a closed_disappeared fact went back to open_order.
   - P5: an old-shape key and the new ord: key were both open for one line (12 + 12 units). The orphan retire is limited to the same job, so it does not guard this. A dated newer job does close the old row (control in P5).
   - The "did not close open orders" notice does not say anything was re-opened.

4. Cross-tenant risk. Latent today: cip has one tenant, default, and 0 identity-key collisions.
   - fact_upsert_key is globally unique and has no tenant. Tenant B's shipped load deleted tenant A's fact. Verified by P8.
   - _HISTORY_SQL (shipping_water.py:133-153) joins observations by line_identity_key with no tenant filter. Tenant B's vessel showed slip 60 and first ETA 2026-10-01, taken from tenant A's observation. Verified by probe.
   - Source mapping memory, remembered sheets, cleared tokens and token aliases have no tenant, and 9c4a27d3 now reuses them automatically. Read in code, not executed.

5. Open-book scope gaps.
   - P3: the older-report guard ignores operating unit. A lagging OU's book wrote nothing.
   - P1a: closing only covers the report types the job carries. A newer book with no Unship lines closed no Unship fact.
   - Remembered sheets: a new or renamed sheet is silently left out and validation auto-starts, with no flag.
