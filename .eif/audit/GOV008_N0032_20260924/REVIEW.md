## Accessibility (quality.a11y): FAIL
Measured in the DOM on case 46:
- tablist has `role=tablist` and `aria-label="Case detail"`. OK.
- Tabs have `role=tab` and correct `aria-selected`, with roving tabindex (0 on the active tab, -1 on the rest). OK.
- Keyboard works. Clicking USD pivot and pressing ArrowRight moves focus to Events (`:focus-visible` true). Enter activates it: aria-selected becomes Events:true and the panel switches to `settlement-tabpanel-events`. OK. MUI's default is manual activation, which is acceptable.
- Focus visibility is **weak**. `outline: none`, and the only indicator is MUI's translucent grey focus-ripple circle. It is visible in the zoomed capture but low contrast on the dark theme. This is an observation rather than a fail, but a 2px focus ring would be better.
- **FAIL: tab and panel are not associated, and the tabpanel has no accessible name.** Every tab has `id=""` and `aria-controls=null`. The `role="tabpanel"` Box (`SettlementCaseTabs.tsx:52`) has no `id`, `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label`. ARIA 1.2 requires an accessible name for tabpanel, and the APG tabs pattern requires the tab↔panel association. A screen-reader user entering the panel hears no name for it. Fix: give each Tab `id={`case-tab-${key}`}` and `aria-controls="case-tabpanel"`, and give the panel `id="case-tabpanel"` and `aria-labelledby={`case-tab-${tab}`}`. The panel should also be focusable (`tabIndex={0}`) when its content starts with no focusable element (for example the pivot table).
- Pivot table: `aria-label="USD pivot by row and column"`, column `<th scope=col>` and row `<th scope=row>` including the Total row. OK. Minor: the top-left corner `<th>` is empty. It should be labelled, for example "Period", or be a `<td>`.
- Loading and error states use the correct roles through ModuleDataSection (code).
