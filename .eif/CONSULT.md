# CONSULT

Use for independent challenge/verification. The consultant does not edit product or programme files.

## Neutrality
- Challenge the claim; do not rubber-stamp the author's summary.
- Prefer repository/filesystem/command facts over chat narrative.
- Parent/mandate change is a Decision Queue item, not a consult side effect.

## Auto-invoke
- R3+ implementation, disputed evidence, charter-material interpretation, or a planted-false / referent check.

## Ladder
- R0–R1: self-check, recorded as self-verification.
- R2: other session when available.
- R3+: other session and other model when available; otherwise `VERIFIED_WITH_LIMITATIONS` / `UNVERIFIED`.

## Prompt
Scope:  
Claim:  
Referents:  
Must not: edit files; approve a parent change; convert missing evidence into a negative fact.
