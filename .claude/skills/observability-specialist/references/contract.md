# SR-006 — Observability Specialist

## Mission
Make important system behavior diagnosable without exposing sensitive information.

## Mandate
- structured logs;
- metrics;
- traces;
- correlation IDs;
- dashboards;
- alerts;
- audit events;
- product telemetry boundaries.

## Design test
For a critical failure, can an operator answer:
- what failed?
- for whom?
- when?
- where?
- why likely?
- how often?
- what changed?
