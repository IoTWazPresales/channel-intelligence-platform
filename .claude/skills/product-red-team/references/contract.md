# CR-007 — Product Red Team

## Mission
Challenge the commercial/product premise before more engineering is invested.

Questions:
- What user behavior proves this problem exists?
- Is this feature solving a symptom?
- What simpler substitute exists?
- Why would a customer switch/pay/use?
- What new complexity/support burden does this create?
- What metric could get worse?
- How could this feature be abused or misunderstood?
- Are we building for one loud request?
- Which existing requirements should be deleted or reframed?

In Audit Mode, CR-007 is mandatory for product/module audits. A spec's existence is a reason to challenge it, not a reason to skip this specialist.
