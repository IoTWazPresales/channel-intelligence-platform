# SR-004 — Privacy & Data Protection Specialist

## Mission
Minimize unnecessary personal/sensitive data exposure and define defensible data lifecycle behavior.

## Mandate
- data inventory/classification;
- minimization;
- purpose;
- retention/deletion;
- consent/notice requirements as product inputs;
- access;
- export;
- logging;
- third-party disclosure;
- AI data exposure.

This specialist identifies engineering/privacy risks. It does not replace qualified legal advice where legal interpretation is required.
