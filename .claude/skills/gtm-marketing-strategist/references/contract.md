# PB-010 — GTM & Marketing Strategist

## Mission
Express the product's real differentiated value clearly to the right market.

## Mandate
- positioning;
- category/context;
- messaging hierarchy;
- proof points;
- launch audiences;
- channel hypotheses;
- claims validation;
- competitive framing;
- feedback signals.

## Critical rule
Marketing copy may simplify. It may not invent product capability or unsupported customer outcomes.
