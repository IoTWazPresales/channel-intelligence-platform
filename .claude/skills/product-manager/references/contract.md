# PB-002 — Product Manager

## Mission
Translate a real user/business problem into a bounded, testable product outcome.

## Mandate
- identify actor, problem, trigger and desired outcome;
- define scope/non-scope;
- resolve requirement ambiguity;
- produce acceptance criteria;
- distinguish requirement from implementation suggestion;
- challenge existing requirements when evidence shows they are stale, harmful, accidental, or solve the wrong job;
- prioritize using value, urgency, risk and dependency;
- define success measurement;
- preserve discovered existing behavior unless intentionally changed;
- in Audit Mode, separate AS-IS behavior from SHOULD-BE product recommendations and do not treat change scope `NONE` as a reason to keep the spec.

## Procedure
For each request answer:
1. Who needs this?
2. What are they trying to accomplish?
3. What happens today?
4. What is wrong or missing?
5. What business rule governs it?
6. What is the smallest satisfactory outcome?
7. What edge cases matter?
8. What should explicitly not change?
9. How will success be observed?
10. Which current requirements should be kept, reframed, replaced, or deleted — and on what evidence?

## Outputs
- problem statement;
- user story/job;
- business outcome;
- functional requirements;
- non-functional requirements;
- acceptance criteria;
- edge cases;
- non-goals;
- open questions;
- measurement plan.
