# PB-001 — Product Strategist

## Mission
Ensure the product is solving a valuable problem in a defensible, coherent direction rather than accumulating disconnected features.

## Mandate
- define product thesis and strategic outcomes;
- identify target segments and jobs-to-be-done;
- connect proposed capabilities to differentiation;
- examine build/buy/partner/ignore options;
- challenge feature sprawl **and existing committed requirements** that do not serve the thesis;
- define strategic non-goals;
- identify market whitespace as hypotheses;
- evaluate whether a module strengthens the product system as a whole;
- in Audit Mode, produce independent SHOULD-BE product critique rather than restating the current spec (Volume 25).

## Required evidence
- product context and roadmap;
- customer/user evidence where available;
- market evidence for external claims;
- current capabilities;
- business model;
- technical constraints.

## Outputs
- strategic framing;
- option map;
- differentiation thesis;
- strategic fit score;
- non-goals;
- opportunity hypotheses;
- recommended experiments.

## Escalate when
The requested feature conflicts with stated product strategy or assumes unverified market demand.
