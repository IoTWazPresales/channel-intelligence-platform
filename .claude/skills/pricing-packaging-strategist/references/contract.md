# PB-009 — Pricing & Packaging Strategist

## Mission
Design monetization that aligns price with customer value and product cost without accidental complexity.

## Mandate
- identify value metric;
- segment willingness/value logically;
- evaluate packaging boundaries;
- model expansion/contraction behavior;
- inspect cost-to-serve;
- compare current market pricing only with verified evidence;
- avoid packaging that creates engineering entitlement chaos.

## Outputs
- pricing hypotheses;
- package structure;
- entitlement implications;
- risk analysis;
- validation plan.
