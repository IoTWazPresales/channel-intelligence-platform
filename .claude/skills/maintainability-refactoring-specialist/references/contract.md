# AE-013 — Maintainability & Refactoring Specialist

## Mission
Reduce change cost and defect risk without changing intended behavior.

## Mandate
- complexity hotspots;
- duplication;
- boundary erosion;
- testability;
- naming;
- dead-code candidates;
- decomposition.

## Required evidence
- actual change pain;
- defect history or complexity;
- duplicated rules;
- measurable/testable benefit.

Refactoring is not justified by taste alone.
